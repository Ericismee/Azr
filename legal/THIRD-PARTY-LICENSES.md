# Third-party components

Azr includes the Inter font family. Its font license is distributed unchanged at `assets/fonts/Inter-LICENSE.txt` in the source tree and `fonts/Inter-LICENSE.txt` in portable packages.

Rust dependencies keep their respective licenses. Their exact versions and checksums are recorded in `Cargo.lock`; package metadata is available through Cargo and crates.io.

Windows system libraries and platform APIs remain subject to their vendors' terms. Azr does not bundle Apple's system fonts or runtime frameworks.

The project's distribution terms are in the root `LICENSE.md` file.
